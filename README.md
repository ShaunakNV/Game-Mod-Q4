Quake 4 Mod
Shaunak Vaidya
IT 266 - Fall 2026

The temperature bar will increase your speed and acceleration, it will increase gradually as you move
Move too fast into a wall or enemy and you will take damage
Movement abilities will drain your temperature
Land melee abilities successfully to restore your temperature
Keep your temperature above 90 to charge your ult bar

Movement abilties -
1 - Backdash
2 - Lunge
3 - Side dash
4 - Superjump
5 - Temperature disperse - release your built up temperature and send all the enemies around you flying back

Melee abilities -
2 - Lunge Melee - Hit the enemy as close as possible after lunging to maximize damage and throw the enemy back
3 - Side dash Melee - Hit the enemy from directly behind to maximize damage and throw the enemy back
4 - Super slam - Slam on your enemy from above to crush them
9 - Knock back - Throw the enemy back
0 - Life steal - Damage the enemy and heal yourself

Ultimate abilities - 
6 - All enemies are frozen, you are forced to rush forward, hit every enemy in your path so you don't collide with them
7 - All enemies are frozen, click 3 enemies within a couple second and kill them, hit less than 3 and you'll be frozen
8 - All enemies are frozen, sacrifice your health to do a large amount of damage to one enemy

Enemies will spawn in waves every minute
